# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individuals who keep personal account credentials locally and need to unlock, review, and update them without relying on a cloud service.

## Product Purpose

Provide a local encrypted password vault with a clear, secure sign-in flow and safe handling of sensitive account information.

## Brand Personality

Calm, minimal, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, noisy security theatrics, and controls that make standard credential-management tasks difficult to recognize.

## Design Principles

- Make security state and consequences explicit.
- Keep the primary vault workflow focused and low-friction.
- Require deliberate confirmation for security-sensitive preference changes.
- Preserve familiar desktop controls and keyboard navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation and visible standard focus behavior. Do not rely on colour alone to communicate security state. Clipboard copy and cut shortcuts are intentionally unavailable within the application.
